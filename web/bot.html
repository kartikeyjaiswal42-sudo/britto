BrittoBot

BrittoBot reads the public product pages that shoppers ask Britto to track, so it can record their price history.

  • It identifies itself as BrittoBot in every request and never pretends to be a browser.
  • It fetches only single product pages that the store's robots.txt allows.
  • It checks each product at most every few hours, one page at a time, and backs off when a store refuses it.
  • It never works around a store's bot defences. Stores that refuse it are not read by our server.

Store operators: if you want BrittoBot to stop reading your site, block the BrittoBot user agent and it will back off automatically.
