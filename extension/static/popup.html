Britto

Open any product on Amazon.in, Flipkart or Myntra. Britto shows its price history and whether the discount is real, in a panel at the bottom right.

What Britto sends

Only what the product page shows: its address, title, image, price, M.R.P. and whether it is in stock. Never your cookies, account, orders, address or browsing history. Prices you see help every Britto user; a big jump is shown only after a second shopper sees the same.
